01 / ABOUT

I am a researcher in zoology interested in understanding the microbial communities associated with insects and the ways these relationships influence insect biology.

My current academic interests centre on insect gut bacteriomes, microbial diversity, probiotic bacteria and sericulture. I am particularly interested in combining microbiological techniques with modern sequencing approaches to better understand insect–microbe associations.
